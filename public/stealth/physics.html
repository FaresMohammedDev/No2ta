Q6 Question 6: Compare & Test Key Formulas

3 Comparisons

1. Compare: Magnetic Flux (\(\Phi_m\)) vs Magnetic Flux Density (\(B\))

Magnetic Flux (\(\Phi_m\)):
Total number of magnetic field lines passing through an area.
• Unit: Weber (Wb) • Quantity: Scalar
Magnetic Flux Density (\(B\)):
Number of flux lines passing normally through a unit area.
• Unit: Tesla (T) or Wb/m² • Quantity: Vector

2. Laws of Magnetic Field at a Glance

Straight Wire \(B = \frac{\mu I}{2\pi d}\)

Field: Concentric circles around wire.

Circular Coil \(B = \frac{\mu N I}{2 r}\)

Field: Short bar magnet / uniform at center.

Solenoid \(B = \mu n I = \frac{\mu N I}{L}\)

Field: Parallel uniform lines along internal axis.

3. Magnetic Force on Current-Carrying Wire (\(F = BIL\sin\theta\))

✓ Perpendicular (\(\theta = 90^\circ\)): \(F_{\text{max}} = BIL\) ✗ Parallel (\(\theta = 0^\circ\)): \(F = 0\)
